# Status of Kuwait Law No. 103 of 2019 (Auditing Profession Law)

**Question:** Is Law No. 103 of 2019 on the practice of the auditing profession (القانون رقم 103 لسنة 2019 في شأن مزاولة مهنة مراقبة الحسابات) still in force, or has it been repealed, replaced or amended?

**Research date:** 2 November 2026

## Conclusion

**The law is in force. I found no repeal, no replacing law, and no amendment to the law itself.**

- The changes since 2019 have all been to its **executive regulations and ministerial decisions**, not to the law.
- The newest evidence is a Ministry of Commerce and Industry decision dated **10 March 2026**. It cites the law as a current legal basis, **without** the phrase "وتعديلاته" ("and its amendments"). Kuwaiti drafting adds that phrase when a law has been amended.
- **Confidence: high up to March 2026, moderate after that.** I could not search the Official Gazette (Kuwait Al-Youm) directly. Check it, or MOCI's legislation portal, for anything published after March 2026 (see "Gaps").

## 1. Background

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Title | Law No. 103 of 2019 on the Practice of the Auditing Profession |
| How it was passed | Approved by the National Assembly at its 1 July 2019 session (42 for, 15 against), then issued and published in the Official Gazette |
| What it replaced | Decree-Law No. 5 of 1981 on the practice of the auditing profession. Law 103/2019 repealed it. |
| Regulator | Ministry of Commerce and Industry (MOCI), which keeps the registers of auditors |
| Transition | Existing auditors had one year from the issue of the executive regulations to bring their status into line (Art. 31) |

So Law 103/2019 did the repealing: it replaced the 1981 decree-law. Nothing has since repealed it.

## 2. What has changed since 2019: regulations only

All of these are ministerial decisions made **under** the law. None of them amends the law's text.

| Instrument | Subject | Effect on Law 103/2019 |
|---|---|---|
| Decision 384/2019 | Rules and procedures for the auditing licensing exam | Implementing rule |
| **Decision 13/2020** | **Executive Regulations of Law 103/2019** (about 1 Feb 2020, 24 articles) | Implementing regulation |
| Decision 180/2020 | Regulation of professional firms for accounting and auditing | Implementing rule |
| Decision 118/2021 | Amends Decision 180/2020 | Amends a regulation only |
| Decision 111/2022 | Amends the decision forming the committee that investigates auditor violations | Committee rule only |
| Decisions 21/2023 and 144/2023 | Exam rules; 144/2023 amends 21/2023 | Amends a regulation only |
| Decision 159/2023 | Continuing professional development for auditors | Implementing rule |
| Decision 160/2023 | Committee for appeals against disciplinary decisions | Implementing rule |
| **Decision 183/2023** | **Amends the Executive Regulations (Decision 13/2020)** | Amends the regulations, **not the law** |
| Decision 150/2025 (31 Jul 2025) | Forms the permanent technical committee on accounting rules and audit guidance | Cites Law 103/2019 as a current basis |
| Decision 25/2026 (10 Mar 2026) | Matrix of AML/CFT violations and penalties for designated non-financial businesses and professions | Cites Law 103/2019 as a current basis |

## 3. Evidence that the law has not been amended

1. **Decision 150/2025 (31 July 2025).** The preamble cites *"القانون رقم (103) لسنة 2019 في شأن مزاولة مهنة مراقبة الحسابات"* with no "وتعديلاته". In the same preamble:
   - Decree-Law 15/1979 is cited "والقوانين المعدلة له" ("and the laws amending it").
   - The Executive Regulations are cited as amended by Decision 183/2023.

   The drafters marked amendments wherever they existed, but marked none for Law 103/2019.
2. **Decision 25/2026 (10 March 2026).** The same pattern: the law is cited with no amendment, and only the regulations are cited as "المعدل بالقرار 183 لسنة 2023".
3. **Legal databases.** The Kuwaiti legal compilations (lawskw.com, mesferlaw.com) and the Raqib50 parliamentary tracker list the law with its implementing decisions only. None lists an amending or repealing law.
4. **No amending decree-law found.** The National Assembly has been suspended since May 2024, so new legislation is issued as Amiri decree-laws. The decree-laws reported for 2025–2026 cover other subjects, for example:
   - Decree-Law 10/2026: digital commerce
   - Decree-Law 67/2026: the oil sector
   - Decree-Law 78/2026: commercial concealment

   None touches the auditing profession law.

## 4. Things to watch

- **Draft decree-law amending the Companies Law (No. 1 of 2016).** The Cabinet approved it on about 29 September 2026 and sent it to the Amir. As of early October 2026 it had not been issued.
  - The reported changes are dropping notarisation of company documents and abolishing the joint venture (Mohassa) company form.
  - Nothing reported suggests it amends Law 103/2019.
  - The Companies Law does have its own provisions on appointing auditors to companies. If the decree-law is issued, check whether it changes those provisions.
- **"Decree-law" vs "law".** Some secondary sources, including some MOCI pages, call it "Decree-Law No. 103 of 2019". That is a labelling error. It was passed by the National Assembly as an ordinary law. The error does not affect whether the law is in force.

## 5. Gaps and how to close them

- I could not search the full Kuwait Al-Youm archive. A decree-law published after March 2026 could be missing from the web sources I could reach.
- To confirm definitively:
  1. Search Kuwait Al-Youm (or the Fatwa and Legislation Department database) for "103 لسنة 2019" in issues published since March 2026.
  2. Check MOCI's legislation page for the auditing profession (Auditors Affairs Department).
  3. Look at the preamble of the newest MOCI decision on auditors. If it cites Law 103/2019 without "وتعديلاته", the law is still unamended.
