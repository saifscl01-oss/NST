# Spotify Discovery Mode — rules that constrain the album plan
Verified against Spotify support docs, 28 Aug 2026.

## Eligibility — ALL four must hold
1. Released on Spotify 30+ days (earliest release date if multiple versions)
2. **20+ streams in Discovery Mode contexts in the last 28 days**  <- the trap
3. Met Spotify monetization criteria last month = **1,000+ streams in previous 12 months**
   (plus an undisclosed minimum unique-listener count)
4. Distributed by an approved licensor -- **SATISFIED**. Discovery Mode is live on the NST
   S4A dashboard, which cannot happen without a participating licensor. Not a blocker.

Artist-level access: satisfied (74,305 monthly listeners, participating licensor).
**The constraint is entirely track-level.** Criteria 1-3 are what's failing.

## Discovery Mode contexts — exactly three surfaces
Spotify Radio · Autoplay · Spotify Mixes (Daily, Artist, Mood, Decade, Genre)
NOT counted: Discover Weekly, Daylist, Your DJ, Smart Shuffle, On Repeat, Blend,
editorial playlists, listener playlists, on-demand/profile plays.

## Cost
30% commission on recording royalties from streams in those contexts while enrolled.
All other streams uncommissioned.

## Campaign calendar
Set up between the 11th and 00:00 UTC on the last day of a month; campaign starts
00:00 UTC on the 1st of the next month; selections editable until the deadline.

## Applied to NST
DM-context share of streams = (Radio 85,411 + Mixes 11,235) / 147,908 = **65.3%**
(Autoplay not itemised in S4A, so true share >= this)
30% x 65.3% = **19.6% haircut** on blended revenue. $0.00165 -> ~$0.00133 effective.

Break-even lift required on Radio/Mixes streams: **42.9%**
(equivalently ~28% lift on total streams)

| Lift on DM-context streams | Total streams | Revenue | Yr-1 base $354 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0%   | +0%  | -19.6% | $285 |
| 25%  | +16% | -8.2%  | $325 |
| 43%  | +28% | 0%     | $354 |
| 75%  | +49% | +14.7% | $406 |
| 100% | +65% | +26.1% | $446 |

**Absolute cost of DM at zero lift: $69.** Not a money decision at this scale —
take the audience, ignore the 30%.

## Consequences for the plan
- **PEAK cannot be enrolled.** <1,000 streams/12mo fails monetization outright.
  (Also why it has earned $1.09 lifetime.)
- **Against The World is the diagnostic case.** 1,398 lifetime (all within 12mo) should clear
  the 1,000-stream monetization bar. If it clears that and is STILL ineligible, it's failing
  the 20-DM-context-stream bar -- fewer than one algorithmic play a day, on a profile serving
  74,000 monthly listeners. That would mean Spotify is not serving NST-solo material AT ALL.
  Alternative: the undisclosed minimum unique-listener count (ATW had 38 listeners/28d).
  **S4A shows which criterion is unmet. This is the single most informative screen available
  and it decides whether the album is structured as solo or with a feature.**
- **No album track can be enrolled at release.** Day 30 minimum, and only if the
  algorithm already found it. Oct release -> Dec campaign at the earliest.
- **Discovery Mode is an amplifier, not an igniter.** It cannot bootstrap a dead track.

## The loop this explains
credit on a sauvachi release -> served by Radio -> becomes DM-eligible ->
DM amplifies the Radio placement -> compounds.
CARBON and 4 BRICKS DOWN completed the loop. PEAK and Against The World failed at step 1
and so could never reach step 2. The "solo doesn't work" finding and the "Discovery Mode
did it" finding are two steps of the same mechanism, not competing explanations.

## How a track actually earns eligibility
20 DM-context streams in 28 days is what ~15 engaged people do in a week.
NST has 7,786 monthly active listeners and 406 super listeners, streaming at 5.0/listener
vs 2.0 overall -- they drive 26% of streams from 10% of listeners. That group is more than
enough to clear the bar IF the track reaches them at release.
Radio/Autoplay/Mixes are fed by completion rate, save rate and repeat listening -- intensity,
not reach. Saves grew 26.7% last period vs 13.1% stream growth, which is the healthiest
signal in the data and the one that feeds this loop.

Month-one objective for the lead single is not sales or reach: it is clearing 20 algorithmic
streams so month two can compound. Concrete, free levers: super listeners first, pre-saves so
day-one engagement is dense, and sequencing that lets Autoplay carry listeners in from CARBON.
