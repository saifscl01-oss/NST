## The counter-example — enrollment is not the mechanism
Also in the June campaign, same month, same account:
- TUSS: 776 streams all month (below the 500-stream floor for lift reporting)
- CALL YOUR PHONE: 272 streams
- mont blanc: historical lift +63% — DM barely moved it
- To Kojayi Remix: historical lift +123%

DM multiplies whatever response a track already gets. Multiplying ~zero gives ~zero.
Getting all 5 album tracks enrolled is NECESSARY BUT NOT SUFFICIENT.

## Modelling correction this forced
The archetypes are calibrated on CARBON and 4BD, which were DM-enrolled throughout.
So the projected stream volumes ALREADY assume DM-level performance — they were just
priced at the full royalty rate with no commission. Correcting both sides:

| Scenario | With DM (net of 30%) | Without DM | DM worth |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bear | $109 | $70 | +55% |
| Base | **$284** | $183 | +55% |
| Bull | $684 | $440 | +55% |
| Base streams yr1 | 237,000 | 123,000 | +93% |

Headline moves $354 -> **$284**. Unenrolled counterfactual $183.

## Caveats
Lift figures are Spotify's ML counterfactual estimates, generated only for songs with
500+ DM streams in the previous month — so they are conditioned on already-performing
tracks and cannot be read as what a cold track would get. Spotify states they may vary
and are not predictive.
